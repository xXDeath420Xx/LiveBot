Nothing to set up

CertiFried Tycoon works as soon as you activate it as a panel. Viewers start their own garden from the panel; the game keeps it under their Twitch account once they choose to share their Twitch ID.

Seasons, tournaments and the leaderboard run on their own. Server owners on Discord can see their community's players under Manage > Economy & trading > CertiFried Tycoon at certifriedmultitool.com.
