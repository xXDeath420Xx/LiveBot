Fewer bots, and nobody has to learn anything new

Kabbot can take over what your other bots do, and the aim is that it feels like nothing changed: the same commands people already type, the same replies they already read. Here's everything that would be swapped, and the few things worth a decision from you. None of it happens until you say so.

01Four things worth your call

Everything else is ready to go. These four are judgement calls, and they're yours.

1 · Ko-fi

Kabbot can hand out supporter roles automatically, but Ko-fi keeps the key to its own door: there's a token that only you can copy out of your Ko-fi settings. No bot can fetch it, so this one genuinely needs you.

Either: grab the token and Kabbot takes supporter roles over · or: leave Ko-fi Bot where it is and nothing changes.

2 · DISBOARD

DISBOARD does one thing: it lists the server on a public "find a server" site so people can come across it. That's not something we'd replace — it's really a question of whether you want the listing.

Either: keep it and keep the listing · or: let it go and the server stays word-of-mouth.

3 · Blerp

Kabbot has a soundboard — upload clips, get a panel of buttons, they play into voice. The one thing it doesn't have is Blerp's big hosted library of sounds nobody here uploaded.

If people use it for the library, it's worth keeping. If it's really just a few clips, we can bring those across.

4 · Seven spare roles

The tidy-up leaves 7 empty roles going spare. They'd make good game or interest roles — but you know what this server actually plays and we don't. Give us the names and we'll put them on a click-to-get panel.

04Tidying up the roles

There are 106 roles, and a fair few of them aren't doing anything.

This isn't anyone's fault — it's just how Carl-bot works. Every time its role panels got rebuilt, it quietly made a brand-new set of roles and left the old set behind. So there are now six roles called "He/Him", and two sets of regions. Only one set of each has anybody in it; the others have simply been sitting there.

Nobody loses a role

Every role we'd touch has zero people in it. And we wouldn't delete them — we'd rename them into roles the server is missing. A rename keeps the role itself completely intact; it just gives it something to do.

05What happens, in what order

  • Settings first. Counting, logging, the go-live ping, the games. Config only — nothing posted, nothing deleted, nobody notices a thing.
  • Then the roles. The renames above. Nothing with a person in it gets touched.
  • Then the panels. The new click-to-get panels go up, and Carl-bot's old ones come down in the same step, so there's never a moment with two of each sitting there. This is the only part that changes what's actually in a channel, so it's its own separate button.
  • Only then do any bots leave. One at a time, and only once everything that bot has ever posted is accounted for by something Kabbot does.
Why the old panels have to come down. Carl-bot's panels work by reacting to a message. Once Carl-bot leaves, that message still sits there with all its reactions showing — so people carry on clicking them and quietly get nothing. That's worse than the panel being gone. The replacements use buttons, which is also why they'll feel a little snappier.
