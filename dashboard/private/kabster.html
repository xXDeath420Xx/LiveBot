Tidying up the roles

There are 106 roles, and a fair few aren't doing anything.

This isn't anyone's fault — it's just how Carl-bot works. Every time its role panels got rebuilt, it quietly made a brand-new set of roles and left the old set behind. So there are now six roles called "He/Him", and two sets of regions. Only one set of each has anybody in it.

Nobody loses a role. Every role we'd touch has zero people in it. And we wouldn't delete them — we'd rename them into roles the server is missing. A rename keeps the role itself completely intact; it just gives it something to do.

Most of these roles already exist — colours, timezones, Africa. They're empty simply because there has never been a panel to pick them from. So we add three: a colour panel (15, one at a time), a timezone panel (12, one at a time), and an opt-in ping panel (5, tick as many as you like). Carl-bot's original four stay exactly as they behave today.

And Africa comes back: Carl-bot's region panel has a 🦒 button pointing at a role that no longer exists — click it today and nothing happens. The Africa role itself is fine; the button just lost track of it.

What happens, in what order

  • Settings first. Counting, logging, the go-live ping, the games. Config only — nothing posted, nothing deleted, nobody notices a thing.
  • Then the roles. The renames above. Nothing with a person in it gets touched.
  • Then the panels. The new ones go up and Carl-bot's old ones come down in the same step, so there's never a moment with two of each. This is the only part that changes what's in a channel, so it's its own separate button.
  • Only then do any bots leave. One at a time, and only once everything that bot has ever posted is accounted for by something Kabbot does.
Why the old panels have to come down. Carl-bot's panels work by reacting to a message. Once it leaves, that message still sits there with all its reactions showing — so people carry on clicking them and quietly get nothing. That's worse than the panel being gone.
