## Product Overview

RecoCards (https://recocards.com) is a platform for creating **group greeting cards** and **kudos boards**. It offers both free and premium tiers. The platform supports:

- Online group greeting cards (multi-page, visual layout with positioned messages)
- Kudos boards (list/feed-style layout with ordered messages)
- AI-generated personalized cover images
- Gift card/gifting integration (boards only)
- White-label business solutions
- Embeddable cards/boards for websites
- Unlimited signers, messages, and pages

### Key Concepts
- **Card Creator/Owner**: The person who created the card; has full admin control
- **Signer/Contributor**: Anyone who adds a message to a card/board
- **Recipient**: The person the card is intended for (receives via Deliver flow)
- **Invite Link**: Shared with contributors so they can add messages
- **Deliver Link**: Sent to the recipient to view the final card (read-only, no "add message" button)
- **Premium Cards**: Never expire, can hide branding, additional features
- **Free Cards**: May expire after 180 days (policy not yet enforced as of writing)

## Cards & Boards

### Two Product Types

RecoCards has two distinct layout types:

1. **Greeting Cards** - Multi-page visual cards where messages are positioned freely on pages (like sticky notes on a page). Messages can be dragged and dropped. Cover page + content pages + last page.

2. **Boards (Kudos Boards)** - Feed/list-style layout where messages appear in a vertical stream. Messages are ordered and can be pinned/ranked. Support gifting.

### Adding New Pages to Greeting Cards

**New pages are automatically added.** No manual action needed.

- By default, a greeting card gets a **cover page**, **two blank pages**, and a **last page** when created.
- RecoCards provides **unlimited messages, unlimited signers, and unlimited pages**.
- As soon as anyone adds a message on the **last blank page**, a new blank page is automatically appended.

**Example flow:**
1. Create a greeting card -> get cover page, 2 empty pages, last page
2. Someone adds a message on the 2nd empty page
3. A 3rd page automatically gets appended

### Changing Card Design / Customization

RecoCards offers several customization options to personalize a card, including:
- Card cover image
- Heading text
- Font selection
- Other design elements

**How to customize:**

1. **Step 1**: Go to your dashboard and click the card you want to customize
2. **Step 2**: Click the **Customize** button (usually located on the bottom right of the card)
3. **Step 3**: Change the card design per your needs. Make sure to **save your changes**.

**If not signed in:** You should still see the Customize button from the same browser window you created the card from. If it doesn't appear, see the "Deliver Button Not Showing" troubleshooting section.

### Embedding Cards/Boards to Websites

Two options for embedding:

#### Option 1: RecoCards for Business (White Label)
- Available at https://recocards.com/business/
- Provides a **white label solution** with business-specific controls and automations
- Best for ongoing/integrated use cases

#### Option 2: Premium Cards (iframe)
- For one-off use cases
- Use **premium cards** which allow you to **hide the RecoCards branding** from the card's customize menu (under Customize settings)
- Then embed the card/board in an **iframe** on your website

### Overlapping Messages on Greeting Cards

The placement algorithm is designed to avoid overlaps, but they can still happen. Two solutions:

#### 1. Move the Messages
- **Card creators** can **drag and drop** any messages on a card
- Card creators can also **delete** messages
- Click on a message to see options (move, delete)

#### 2. Use Multiple Pages
- RecoCards offers **unlimited pages** on a card
- Move some messages to a different page to reduce crowding
- Messages can be moved to previous/next pages using **<< and >>** buttons

### Turn Off New Messages (Freeze a Card)

To prevent anyone from adding new messages:

1. Open the card
2. Click **Customize**
3. Scroll down to **'More Settings'**
4. Set **Card Status** to **'Frozen'**

Path: `Customize -> Scroll to 'More Settings' -> Card Status -> Frozen`

### Board: Message Ordering / Pinning

#### For Boards:
- Use the **Pin** feature to control message order
- Click the **top right of a message** -> click **Pin**
- Pinned messages can be **ranked** (e.g., 1-10)
- The **default rank** of any message is **100**
- This allows putting messages at the top or bottom as desired
- **Only the board creator** can pin messages

#### For Greeting Cards:
- **Drag and drop** messages to reorder them on a page
- Use **<< and >> buttons** to move messages between pages (previous/next)
- **Who can move messages:**
  - The **card creator** can move any messages
  - The **message creator** can move their own message

### Deleting Messages

#### Greeting Card:
- Click on a message and hit the **'x' button**

#### Board:
- Click on the **top-right of a message**, and hit the **'Delete'** option

**Who can delete:**
- A **message creator** (signer) can delete their own message
- The **card/board creator** can delete any message

### Message Retention / Card Expiration

- **Premium cards** (including boards) **do not expire**
- **Free cards** may expire after **180 days**
- The 180-day limit is **not currently enforced** but will be in the near future
- This is a resource-saving measure being considered

### Signer Limits

**Unlimited.** There is no limit on how many people can add messages (sign) on a card or board.

### Deliver vs Invite

These are two distinct flows with different purposes:

#### Invite (for contributors)
- Use the **'Invite'** button
- Purpose: Send link to people so they can **add a message** to the card/board
- Recipients of the invite link see the "add message" UI

#### Deliver (for the final recipient)
- Use the **'Deliver'** button
- Purpose: Send the completed card/board to the **intended recipient**
- The delivered link is **read-only** -- recipients see the card but **cannot add new messages**

**CRITICAL: Do NOT use the Deliver button to invite contributors.** When you use the Deliver button, the link sent does not allow adding new messages. If you accidentally invite people via the Delivery flow, they will be stuck and unable to add messages.

### Cancel or Reschedule Delivery

- Click the **'Deliver'** button on the card to manage delivery
- Options include **cancelling** or **rescheduling** delivery
- **Note:** Cards already delivered cannot be un-delivered
- However, you can **re-deliver** or **delete** delivered cards if needed

### Deliver Button Not Showing

**Issue:** You created a card/board but the Deliver/Customize buttons are missing.

**Troubleshooting steps:**

1. **Make sure you're signed in** -- Without signing in, there's no way to associate card ownership with your account.

2. **Open the card in the same browser** where you first created it -- If you created a card without signing in, a temporary token is saved in your browser. Once you sign in from that same browser, the token auto-associates with your account.

3. **Make sure your link is correct** -- There are two types of links:
   - **Creator/collaborator link** (shows Deliver/Customize buttons):
     - Boards: `https://recocards.com/board/my-board-66547516750`
     - Greeting cards: `https://recocards.com/greeting-card/my-greeting-card-34892`
   - **Recipient link** (read-only, NO edit buttons):
     - Boards: `https://recocards.com/view/b/my-board-66547516750`
     - Greeting cards: `https://recocards.com/view/gc/my-greeting-card-62990534184`
   - Note the **`view/gc`** and **`view/b`** in recipient URLs -- these are read-only views

4. **Try the card troubleshooter**: https://recocards.com/self-service/card-troubleshooter

5. **Contact support**: help@recocards.com

### Cannot Download a Card

**Issue:** Download fails with an error message.

**Troubleshooting steps:**

1. **Make sure your card is loaded** -- Wait for all images to fully load before attempting download
2. **Try a different browser** -- Browser/OS rendering issues are the most common cause; most users resolve it this way
3. **Try a different device** -- If browser change doesn't help
4. **Try a different network** -- Some networks/VPNs block parts of the website; try home wifi, turn off VPN, or use cellular
5. **Contact support**: print-help@recocards.com (dedicated print/download support email)

### Character Limits

- There is a **suggested soft limit of 350 characters** per message on greeting cards
- This is **NOT a hard limit** -- contributors can write longer messages
- The 350-character guideline exists to:
  - Encourage concise, impactful messages
  - Ensure optimal formatting and readability

**No hard limit is imposed.** Contributors who wish to write longer messages can exceed the suggestion freely. This ensures flexibility for heartfelt messages on special occasions (retirements, celebrations, farewells, etc.).

**Alternative for very long messages:** Split thoughts across multiple entries on the card for:
- Better organization and readability
- Unique formatting/designs for each entry

## Account Issues

### Reset Password

#### Case 1: Forgot your password
1. Click **'Login'** on the website
2. Click **'Forgot password'**
3. Follow the prompts to reset via email

#### Case 2: Forgot your email
- If **already logged in**: Go to `Dashboard -> My Account -> Email` to see/reset your email
- If **not logged in**: Create a new account with a new email

### Password Reset Email Not Received

**Common causes:**
- The reset email landed in your **spam folder** -- check spam
- Your **email provider or company** is blocking emails from @recocards.com -- ask them to allow-list it

**Workarounds by situation:**

1. **You remember your current password**: Login, go to `Dashboard -> My Account`, change your email address
2. **You are already logged in**: Go to `Dashboard -> My Account`, change your email address
3. **Not logged in + don't remember password**: Only options are:
   - Contact your email provider to understand why they're blocking RecoCards emails
   - Create a new account with a different email (you will **lose access to existing cards**)

### Manage Premium Subscription

**To cancel or change your premium subscription:**

1. Click **Dashboard** from the header section
2. Click **'My Account'** from the sidebar
3. Scroll to the bottom of the page -- subscription management options are there

**Important note:** All cancellations may take up to **48 hours** to be fully processed. If charged after cancelling, the payment should be automatically refunded. If not, contact support.

### Change Email

1. **Login** with your current email and password at https://recocards.com/
2. Go to `Dashboard -> My Account`
3. Change your email address from there

## RecoCards AI

### AI-Generated Cover Images

RecoCards AI is based on a **trained LLM model** capable of generating **personalized cover images**. It can make mistakes -- here's how to fix them:

#### 1. Tune Your Prompt
The quality heavily depends on prompt clarity and specificity:
- **Be Specific**: Clearly state what you want -- colors, themes, specific elements. Instead of "Create a fun cover," say "Create a fun cover with vibrant colors and a playful font."
- **Include Key Details**: Mention specific text, its placement. More detail = less room for error.
- **Use Examples**: Refer to similar designs or styles to help the AI understand your aesthetic.

#### 2. Review and Edit the Output
After generation, review carefully:
- **Text Editing**: Correct spelling mistakes or wording directly in the design tool
- **Design Adjustments**: Tweak font size, color, or image placement

#### 3. Experiment with Variations
- Generate **multiple versions** of your design
- Each variation has slight differences
- You may find a better version without extensive edits

#### 4. Save and Reuse Successful Prompts
- Save prompts that produce good results for future use
- Build a **library of successful prompts** that can be tweaked for new covers
- Ensures consistency across projects and saves time

## Gifts

### How to Enable Gifts

#### Kudos Boards
1. Go to your board
2. Click the **Customize** button
3. Scroll down to **'More Settings'**
4. Enable gifting from the **'Gifting' drop-down**

#### Greeting Cards
**Greeting cards do NOT currently support gifts.** Gifts are a boards-only feature.

### How to Redeem Gifts

Only the **recipient** of a card can redeem gifts, through the **unique link sent to their email**.

#### Step 1 - Deliver the Card
- Use the **Deliver** button to deliver the card to the intended recipient
- Cards can **only be delivered by the card owner/creator**

#### Step 2 - Recipient Receives the Card via Email
- An email is sent to the recipient with a **"View Your Card"** button

#### Step 3 - Recipient Claims the Gift
- When the recipient opens the card via the email link or their unique link, they see a **"Claim Gifts"** button
- The **Claim Gifts** button is only visible if there are gifts associated with the card

#### Gift Delivery
- Once the recipient claims their gifts, they are **delivered within a few days**
- Delivery can take up to **14 additional days** if fraud verification steps are required
